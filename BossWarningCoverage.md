# Boss Warning Coverage

This tracker covers screen reader warnings in `BossAttackWarningSystem`.

Status definitions:

- Done: the boss has at least one spoken warning for a move, phase, proximity danger, or dangerous projectile.
- Needs tuning: the boss has coverage, but live gameplay testing should refine timing, wording, or cooldowns.
- Not done: no boss-specific warning coverage exists yet.
